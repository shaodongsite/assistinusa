Pricing

Fair and transparent — just enough to keep the wheels turning. Every task quoted individually, no surprises after the fact.

1
Fair & Transparent — Just Enough to Get By
My pricing philosophy is fair and transparent. No price gouging, no hidden traps. I'm a retired guy supplementing my income, not running a profit center.
2
Discuss First, Set Scope
Before any work begins, we communicate fully to clarify your expectations and what's realistically achievable — including how, when, and to what standard. This avoids disputes later.
3
Per-Task Billing — Pay Before I Start
Each request is billed separately. One task, one payment — no bundles, no lock-ins. Work begins only after payment is confirmed.
4
Based on Florida Minimum Wage
My base rate is Florida's official minimum wage, plus any actual travel or out-of-pocket costs (if applicable). Small tasks? Even $5–6 is fine.
5
Itemized Quotes — No Hidden Fees
All costs — including extras like transport, postage, or admission fees — are disclosed upfront in the quote. I will never add charges after the fact.
